- [x] 3.5 Add/extend `GameRoom.integration.test.ts` covering: a majority vote sets `skipConfirmationAvailable` without advancing the segment; admin confirmation advances the segment and clears skip votes and `skipConfirmationAvailable`; confirmation at the week's last segment enters week-end; a vote short of majority never enables confirmation; a disconnect that drops the majority clears `skipConfirmationAvailable` and a subsequent confirm attempt is rejected; confirmation is rejected from a non-admin; confirmation is rejected when nothing is awaiting it; a vote is rejected in normal mode; a vote is rejected outside the active phase.
